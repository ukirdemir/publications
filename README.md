## Research projects

- **LOEWE TRIO** (Justus Liebig University, 2025-present): data monitoring networks and database management for intercropping field systems; hydrological and physics-based modelling combined with FFNN, LSTM and novel deep-learning architectures. Includes development of a Python package for the SWIM3 hydrological model (manuscript in preparation).
- **TUBITAK 114Y716** (2018-2022): "Examining Irrigation and Drinking Water Sufficiency Under Climate Change Scenarios in the Future: Case of Gediz Basin". Statistical downscaling, bias correction, multi-model ensembling, Bayesian uncertainty reduction, drought projections.
